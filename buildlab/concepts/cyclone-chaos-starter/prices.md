## 2. The 6-link body (ESTIMATES, 6L prices are not in the snapshot)
- Tabula Rasa: ~1-3 div. Free 6L, no stats. Good for levelling and first maps. Not in the snapshot.
- Fated / drop 6L unique or rare with life: ~5-12 div. Rare 6L with 90+ life and res: ~8-15 div.
- Vorici (chromatic + jeweller + fusing on a white 6S base): roughly 3-8 div in fusings at week one. Variance is high.
- Best path: Tabula to map T10-ish, then a 6L life base (Vorici or bought, ~8 div) once the rest is done.

## 4. Top 3 purchases per divine, and substitutes
1. Life + res on rare gear (helm/gloves/boots/rings): ~15 div total, the biggest survivability gain.
2. Flat phys weapon (or Innsbury + shield, ~1-2 div, best value of the build). Weapon is the damage core.
3. Body 6L: Tabula -> life 6L, 8-10 div. Links raise the damage most.
Substitutes: Tabula for a rare 6L body; Innsbury for a 6 div rare; Ming's Heart (0.02) for a belt (-25% life, boss stage only); Stygian/Heavy Belt for a crafted one; non-influenced rares for influenced ones.

## 5. Levelling / pre-key path, ~1-5 div
- Any 1H sword/sceptre/claw rare with flat phys: 0.2-0.5 div; Tabula 1-3 div (or skip with a 4L).
- Cyclone from 28, Withering Touch from 38, Innsbury from 47. Gems cost 0.02-0.5 div each.
- Life flask, Granite flask, Jade: ~0.1 div. Rare boots with movement speed: ~0.3 div.
- Rings/amulet with life + res: 0.2-0.5 div each. Resist budget 1-2 div in total.
- Total: about 2-5 div, rest saved for the 6L, weapon and rares.
